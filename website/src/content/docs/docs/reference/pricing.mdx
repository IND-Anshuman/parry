This table is **generated from `src/cost.rs` at build time**, so it is always
the exact table compiled into the release you are reading about. Prices are
standard-tier, short-context rates in USD per million tokens.

<Aside type="tip" title="Something out of date?">
Open a [pricing issue](https://github.com/IND-Anshuman/parry/issues/new?template=pricing.yml)
with a link to the provider's pricing page. In the meantime, override any price
with `guard.set_pricing(model, input_per_million, output_per_million)`.
</Aside>

## How rows are matched

Rows match by **longest prefix on a `-`, `@` or `:` boundary**. Dated snapshots
therefore resolve to their family, while snapshots with their own price get
their own row:

- `gpt-4o-2024-08-06` → `gpt-4o`
- `gpt-4o-2024-05-13` → its own row
- `claude-opus-4-1-20250805` → `claude-opus-4-1`
- `gpt-4.1` is **not** matched by `gpt-4`, and `o10` is not matched by `o1`
